How to shoot a 360°
  1. Keep the phone still and turn the object. Prop the phone on a stand or a stack of books, put the object on a lazy Susan, a swivel stool or a plate, and film one slow turn. Turning a bit more than once is fine: the app finds where one full turn ends and keeps exactly that. A plate or turntable that stands out from the background may be kept as part of the product, so pick one close to the background's colour.
  2. No turntable? Turn the object by hand about 10–15° at a time and take a photo after each turn, phone not moving, then drop all the photos. They're ordered by file name.
  3. Walking around works too, but it's the hardest: keep the phone at the same height and tilt the whole way. The app evens out distance and position, but it can't undo a change in viewing angle.
  4. Use a plain background and even light, and keep hands and other moving things out of the shot.
